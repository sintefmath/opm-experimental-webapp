Open Porous Media

OPM Flow in the browser

OPM Flow is the open-source reservoir simulator of the Open Porous Media project. Here its C++ code is compiled to WebAssembly and runs on your own computer, on several threads where the browser allows it. Pick an example or upload a deck, run it, and look at the results in 2D and 3D. No data leaves your machine.

An experimental port by Kjetil Olsen Lye, SINTEF. About WebAssembly: webassembly.org · MDN guide · Emscripten, the compiler used here.
